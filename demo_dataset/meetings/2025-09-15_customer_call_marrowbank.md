# Customer Call: Marrowbank Clinics
Date: 2025-09-15
Attendees: Grace Liu, Tomas Rivera (Support), and the Marrowbank operations lead

## Context
Marrowbank Clinics runs 42 clinics and uses about 600 Kestrin hubs to monitor fridge temperatures for vaccines.

## What they raised
- Alerts arrive late at night because the SMS gateway batches messages.
- They want a weekly PDF report per clinic instead of one combined report.
- They asked whether the hubs keep logging during a network outage. Answer: yes, each hub buffers up to 72 hours of readings and uploads them when the connection returns.
