# Design Review: Wrenfield Alert Rules
Date: 2025-11-20
Attendees: Hiro Tanaka, Elena Rossi (Design), Grace Liu

## Proposal
Let customers set alert rules per site instead of one rule for the whole account.

## Feedback
- Grace: most support tickets about alerts come from customers with mixed sites (clinics and warehouses).
- Elena: keep the rule editor to one screen; advanced options behind a toggle.
- Hiro: rules evaluate on the hub, so they keep working offline.

## Decision
Build per-site alert rules for Wrenfield 3.4. Elena delivers final designs by 5 December.
