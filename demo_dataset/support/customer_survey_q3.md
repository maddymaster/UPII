# Customer Survey Q3 2025: Summary
Prepared by: Grace Liu, 2025-10-20
Responses: 118 customers

Pairing new hubs takes too long: 46% of respondents named it, more than any other issue.

## What customers like
- Reliable temperature alerts.
- The Wrenfield dashboard map view.

## What they asked for
