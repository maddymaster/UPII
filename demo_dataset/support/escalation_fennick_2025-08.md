# Escalation: Fennick Freight
Date: 2025-08-21
Owner: Grace Liu

## Problem
Hubs at three Fennick depots went offline every night between 01:00 and 03:00.

## Cause
The depots switch off their Wi-Fi access points overnight to save power. The hubs were not configured to fall back to cellular.

## Fix
Tomas Rivera enabled cellular fallback on all 120 Fennick hubs on 22 August. No gaps since.

## Outcome
Fennick renewed for one year on 29 August.
