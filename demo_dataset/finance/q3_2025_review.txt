Quarterly Review, Q3 2025
Prepared by Lena Fischer, 2025-10-06

Revenue for Q3 was $1.31M, up 12% on Q2.
Hardware gross margin was 41%; subscription gross margin was 78%.
Operating expenses were flat apart from hosting, which ran over budget in August and September.
Cash at the end of September: $4.2M, before the Series A funds arrived.
