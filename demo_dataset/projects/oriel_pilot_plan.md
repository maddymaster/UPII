# Oriel Schools Trust Pilot Plan
Date: 2025-09-03
Owners: Omar Haddad (commercial), Grace Liu (success)

## Scope
200 Kestrin hubs across 12 schools, monitoring classroom temperature and air quality, free for 60 days from 15 September.

## Success criteria
- Hubs online at least 98% of the time.
- Facilities staff use the Wrenfield dashboard at least weekly.
- The trust agrees to a paid contract by the end of the pilot.
