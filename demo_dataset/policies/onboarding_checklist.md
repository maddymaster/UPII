# New Hire Onboarding Checklist
Owner: Ines Moreau. Last updated: 2025-09-01

## First day
- Collect your laptop and hardware security key from IT.
- Set up single sign-on and the password manager.

## First week
- Meet your onboarding buddy every day.
- Read the engineering handbook or your team's handbook.

## Deadlines
- Complete security training within 14 days of your start date.
- Complete the product tour of Kestrin and Wrenfield within 30 days.
