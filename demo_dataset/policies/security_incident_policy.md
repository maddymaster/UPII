# Security Incident Policy
Owner: Nina Petrova. Last updated: 2025-08-01

- Anyone who suspects a security incident reports it in the #security channel or to the on-call engineer immediately.
- The incident lead is the on-call engineer until the security lead takes over.
- Customers affected by a confirmed data incident are told within 72 hours.
- Every incident gets a written postmortem within 5 working days.
