# Travel Policy
Owner: Lena Fischer. Last updated: 2025-07-01

- Book economy class for flights under six hours.
- Hotel limit: $180 per night, or $250 per night in major cities.
- Meals: up to $60 per day while travelling.
- Book travel at least 14 days ahead where possible.
- Taxis and rideshares are fine between the airport and the hotel; keep the receipts.
