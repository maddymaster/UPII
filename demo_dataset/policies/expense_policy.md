# Expense Policy
Owner: Lena Fischer. Last updated: 2025-07-01

- Submit expenses within 30 days, with a receipt for anything over $25.
- Your manager approves expenses up to $1,000.
- Expenses over $1,000 need approval from the Head of Finance (Lena Fischer).
- Software subscriptions need IT approval first, whatever the amount.
- Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.
