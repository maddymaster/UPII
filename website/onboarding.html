The sovereignty contract

Before anything is captured, know exactly what Smritium will and will never do.

  • Embeddings are computed on this machine
  • Vectors and metadata live in local storage you can inspect
  • Ambient captures wait in an approval inbox until you say yes
  • Every answer cites the exact chunks it came from
  • No corpus byte is uploaded to any cloud, ever, by default
  • No telemetry without an explicit opt-in
