Research

Sovereign memory, measured.

Smritium began as UPII — the Unified Personal Intelligence Interface — a research effort asking one question: what is the minimum architecture that delivers cloud-grade recall while the substrate never leaves the edge? We publish our benchmarks, evals, and reproducibility audits so you can verify, not trust.

The ELEVATE Nxt grant

Smritium's research substrate (UPII) is developed by DataFrontier Innovations Pvt Ltd with support from the ELEVATE Nxt programme, Government of Karnataka — the state's flagship startup grant, administered under the Karnataka Startup Policy through KITS.

The grant funds the science: benchmark infrastructure, retrieval-quality evaluation, deterministic-ingestion hardening, consent-gated ambient connectors, attributed synthesis with verification, cross-platform signed releases, local-network multi-device sync, and a design-partner pilot. Milestone completion reports and demonstration artifacts are produced for each tranche.

Tranche 1 · Months 1–6
Foundations & proof

Performance baselines, chunk-determinism verification, Context Rehydrator eval baseline, local knowledge-graph extraction, provisional patent filing.

Tranche 2 · Months 7–12
Product & pilot

Hardened ambient connectors, citation-accuracy eval + abstention, signed v1.0 installers (macOS/Windows/Linux), LAN multi-device sync, 5–10 user design-partner pilot.

Beyond
The moat

MCP memory bridge, the Membrane (egress audit + redaction), enterprise compliance offering.
